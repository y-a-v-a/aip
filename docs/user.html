1. Signing in

The app is invitation-only. The admin adds your phone number or email address and sends you a personal login link, usually by WhatsApp or email.

  1. Open the link on the phone or computer you want to use.
  2. You see a short welcome and a Sign in button. Tap it.
  3. That’s it. The app remembers this device for a year, so next time you just open the site.

Why the extra tap? WhatsApp and many mail apps open links in the background to build a preview. The link is only used up when you tap Sign in, so a preview can’t spoil it.

Another device? Each link works once. To use the app on a second phone or computer, either sign in there with your PIN (if you have one) or ask the admin for a new link.

Signing in with a PIN

If the admin gave you a PIN, go to the login page and enter your phone number or email and the PIN. Phone numbers can be typed with spaces or dashes — +31 6 1234 5678 works. Emails are not case sensitive. Leave Keep me signed in on this device ticked unless you are on a shared computer.

3. Making a recipe

The recipe only ever uses foods from the ingredient list (plus water), in metric units and °C. If your wish needs something that isn’t on the list, the recipe says so and offers the closest on-list version.

Getting the same kind of dish too often? Leave the wish empty: the app then builds the dish around a randomly chosen protein (lunch/dinner) or fruit (dessert) from the list.

4. Saved recipes

Saved lists every recipe the app has made, newest first, with the meal type and date. Tap one to read it again. Recipes are shared: everyone with access sees the same list.

5. The ingredient list

Ingredients shows the list of foods the AI is allowed to use — one per line. Edit it and press Save ingredients. The next recipe uses the new list straight away.

  • Bullets (•, -, *), blank lines and duplicates are cleaned up automatically.
  • Up to 200 items, each up to 80 characters.
  • The list can’t be saved empty.

The list is shared. Changing it changes what everyone’s recipes can contain.

6. Logging out

Log out ends your session and makes this device forget you. To get back in you need your PIN or a new link from the admin — so on your own phone you normally never need to log out.

7. Troubleshooting

You seeWhat it meansWhat to do
“This login link is invalid, already used, or expired” The link was used before, is older than 7 days, or the admin made a newer one. Ask the admin for a new link.
“Invalid phone number / email or PIN” Wrong PIN, or you don’t have a PIN (link-only account). Check the number/email and PIN, or ask for a link.
“Too many attempts. Try again in a few minutes.” 5 wrong PINs in a row lock that account for 5 minutes. Wait 5 minutes.
“Session expired, please try again.” The page was open a long time and its security token expired. Reload the page and submit again.
Suddenly back on the login page You logged out on this device, cleared your cookies, or the admin signed you out / removed you. Use your PIN or ask the admin for a new link.
A red error after pressing Generate The AI service had a problem (network, credit, or it declined the request). Try again; if it keeps happening, tell the admin.
